# Scheduler portal contract follow-ups

This is a read-only contract comparison of the imported [RM10 v1.1 specification](../../../planning/scheduler/sources/CISLIB_RM10_Application_Scheduler_and_Triggers_Specification_v1.1.md), the approved [R07 design](wireframes.md), and [scheduler contract reconciliation](scheduler-contract-reconciliation.md). The imported source remains SHA-256 `cbb75ceec89564cefeebb003ab51eed565c2bbcad4e18235954accad97c36510`; the repeated attachment introduces no source revision delta.

The attachment was supplied to identify contracts, explicitly excluding implementation of the Scheduler itself. These follow-ups preserve the future requirements; they authorize no calendar producer, scheduler store, worker adapter, recurrence loop, new deployment tooling or background runtime. Existing passive DTO/client checks and approved screenshots are not evidence that those services exist.

Protected and missing evidence are different: a denied/expired/redacted field retains that declared state; **Not reported** is appropriate for unavailable historical evidence, not a substitute for an access decision. Status access never grants captured input/settings/result access. Future changes must retain the exact integer, timestamp, byte/depth, original-key and independent cleanup semantics already frozen in the portable contracts.

The existing reconciliation also records the separate discovery compatibility gap: current v1 ServiceInfo/API-capability representations cap entries at64. The current72-operation canonical OpenAPI plus the one existing application-query feature alias implies73 future feature entries; this is a contract-derived estimate, not an installed-handler observation. The selected service has42 entries after the Configuration companions. Resolve that bounded discovery contract before installing every future handler; neither an SDK-only limit increase nor truncation is acceptable. Nothing in this review changes the legacy limit or advertises uninstalled endpoints.

## What can honestly be shown before a Scheduler backend exists

Existing real Applications/Instances and RM08 definition/source/publication/release/assignment/adoption data may be shown under their actual native permissions and delivered operations. Existing Jobs inventory may show its selected report/registration, names, declared modes, observations and reported application version; it is telemetry, not trusted schema authority, a configured Scheduled Task, a durable Event queue or execution history.

The Scheduler-dependent Task, Event and Run surfaces must report **unsupported/unavailable** until the corresponding real operations exist. Do not display an empty history as proof of no runs, synthesize Tasks from inventory, compute authoritative calendar instants in the browser, enable Publish and apply/Run now/Cancel without the native operations, or substitute synthetic design records into production. Exact typed in-memory field validation may be developed against actual owner descriptions, but it cannot claim semantic owner acceptance, executable activation, calendar production or durable admission.

The approved R07 synthetic previews remain usable as clearly labelled design evidence. They do not establish delivery of history, compatible worker recovery or a scheduler service.

## Approval boundary

No additional product clarification or screenshot gate is needed to preserve the already approved Task-level selectors/mapping, exactly Trigger and Job tabs, separate History, scope-aware Configuration workflows, inherited identity and mixed-version attempt/protected-detail presentation. The follow-ups above bind the data behind those accepted surfaces. Qualifying an exact source/run/revision or displaying an existing unknown/unsupported state does not create a new interaction.

RM10-UI-24 still applies if future work introduces a new interaction, a material layout departure or an execution-identity/history surface not covered by the approved R07 package. That limited future review obligation remains intact; this document grants no new visual or Scheduler runtime authority.
