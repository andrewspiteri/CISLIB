# Deterministic concurrency qualification

WP07-S01-T03 maps all seven original concurrency areas to [exact executable methods](../../../conformance/logging/concurrency-qualification.json): blocked I/O, bounded flush coordination, wall-clock corrections, stop races, already-cancelled waits, pending gauges and invalid-event loss. This builds on the actual queue/flush/stop fixtures implemented with their behaviors and the public candidate/physical cases from WP07-S01-T02.

Three new observations close two gaps. With no admitted events,64 barrier-released invalid direct/lazy calls produce64 global invalid losses,32 factory calls and no output admissions or bytes. An earlier held flush retains invalid0/no loss; a later flush and stop have target0 and succeed while reporting the64 losses. The cases run with one and two enabled outputs. A public-gauge case observes Pending1,3,2,3,0 across first-write release, refill and shared stop. A factory held across closure returns Stopped; None does not invoke a stopped factory. The frozen diagnostic snapshot remains unchanged and the final accepted partition is exact.

Cancellation detaches only its caller. Even an already-cancelled FlushAsync registers a nondroppable target before checking that caller token; the test checks the retained target has no live caller attachment and creates no caller timer. Existing stress cases prove bounded target storage and detach5000 sequential cancelled/expired callers, as well as concurrent cohorts. Controlled monotonic clocks, writer/callback entered signals and explicit releases prove ordering; finite guards detect hangs rather than prove that a race happened.

The qualification includes existing source-reference child processes and controlled internal compositions; the earlier candidate-package OS/file/pipe tests remain complementary public-boundary evidence. Private continuation/storage probes are pinned managed-runtime tests; seeded exhaustion does not represent emitted history. No new production API, dependency, scheduler or general test framework is introduced. Coverage, discovery/applicability, architecture, mutation, cumulative compatibility and complete release/platform qualification remain separate roadmap obligations. [Retained evidence](../../../planning/logging/evidence/wp07-s01-t03/verification.json) records the executed lanes, exact sources/reports, and review outcome.
