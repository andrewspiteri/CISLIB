# Shutdown lifecycle qualification

WP05-S10-T05 adds real consumer processes and explicit barriers over actual Core queues and file ownership. The shipping runtime is unchanged. Development fixtures use the exact compiled Core assembly; package/release qualification remains separate.

Four public-factory modes cover normal file drain, physical console/file drain, producer/stopper races and already-cancelled initiation. The dual case independently captures actual stdout, compares its records with the file and observes application stdout after disposal. The race holds a lazy factory after initial interest and before final admission while eight producers and sixteen stop/dispose callers share a concurrent-start gate. Producer accepted/stopped counts are recorded; no particular producer/stopper interleaving is required. The held factory guarantees the capture-versus-closure race separately. Its held capture must return Stopped, and accepted tickets must match physical file order. All modes preserve a pre-existing seed record.

Four kernel modes combine real FileOutputOwner handles, an exclusive group lease and actual unbuffered FileStream writes with controllable writer barriers. They cover noncooperating writes/flushes, sixty-four cancelled caller waits and a late write failure. The original 9 ms/1 ms deadline split, sixty-four disposal joiners and one real Core deadline callback prove no new drain window. Five queued entries become abandoned while one write remains pending. Their flush confirmation is Failed/unavailable already; this does not mean the active write has settled. A held flush instead has all three records written, zero pending records and a Pending flush confirmation. The actual output-owned CancellationToken.IsCancellationRequested and callback count must remain false/zero after caller cancellations and again at 9 ms, then is observed only at the original deadline while the writer remains held. No timer or cancellation request is asserted to force an OS operation to stop.

These barriers control the asynchronous writer around actual physical files; they do not claim a native FileStream operation was forced to block inside the OS. The kernel modes write through the real owner directly and bypass FileOutputWriter rendering/size/rotation and JSONL startup-boundary inspection. Successful public-factory consumers separately qualify the supported physical composition; they do not inject or observe an OS failure. Together these keep real physical ownership/drain evidence separate from the controlled noncooperating-writer boundary. Owned handles remain held until actual writer completion, then close once. The seed bytes remain intact; cached stop observations stay unchanged while later live diagnostics reflect success or failure.

Three crash modes let a parent kill one exact child after its validated checkpoint: six accepted records held before any new physical byte; a physical 32-byte record prefix with five queued entries; and the same partial write after a bounded deadline. No graceful cleanup is invoked. A second real public-factory child reopens the group. Clean files preserve the seed and append a restart record. Partial files preserve the entire prior active segment as damaged and append to a fresh active segment. Child PIDs, exact assemblies, kill exits and physical before/active/damaged bytes are retained independently. The absent buffered records are asserted only for this controlled schedule. Acceptance, writing and flushing provide no exactly-once or crash-durable guarantee.
