## Verification limits

These are development acceptance and preservation checks, not publication, a new platform-support claim or full release qualification. The original roadmap remains paused. Mutation, historical compatibility and the Linux release lane require their separate obligations at an authorized release boundary. Source graph queries are navigation evidence; compiler completeness, schema version and hardware atomic counters are not inferred. Measured timings describe this host and workload and impose no invented SLA.
